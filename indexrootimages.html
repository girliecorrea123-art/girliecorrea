My Campaign DNA

Optimize before rebuilding.

When performance moves outside KPI, I first diagnose where the bottleneck is instead of automatically killing the entire campaign.

Preserve what works.
Isolate what doesn't.
Test before rebuilding.
Weak CTR
Investigate hook and creative. Launch fresh creative inside the existing campaign when structure is still sound.
Healthy CTR + expensive conversion
Investigate the post-click, form, Messenger or sales funnel.
Frequency ↑ + CTR ↓
Investigate fatigue and refresh creative rather than rebuilding blindly.
CPM ↑ + response stable
Investigate delivery or auction pressure before blaming creative.
One weak ad
Fix or replace the component—not automatically the whole campaign.
Repeated tests fail
When enough evidence shows weak economics across components, consider restructuring.
